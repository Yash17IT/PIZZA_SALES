# Pizza Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes pizza orders and sales patterns to provide actionable business insights. The dashboard identifies peak ordering times, popular pizza types, and sales performance to help restaurant managers optimize operations and marketing strategies.

## Objectives
1. Identify peak and lowest order times during the day.
2. Determine which day of the week has the highest number of orders.
3. Calculate total sales revenue.
4. Identify the most ordered pizza.
5. Identify which pizza generates the most sales.
6. Analyze ordering patterns by time of day (morning/afternoon/evening/night).
7. Determine the top-selling pizza category.

## Data Sources
- Pizza order dataset including time, day, pizza type, quantity, and sales.

## Features
- Interactive dashboard with time-based and category-based visualizations.
- KPI cards showing total sales, top pizzas, and peak hours.
- Trend charts for order volume by time and day.
- Insights into sales performance and customer preferences.

## How to Open
1. Download the `PizzaSalesAnalytics.pbix` file.
2. Open it in **Power BI Desktop (Free Version)**.
3. Explore the dashboards and filters to analyze the data.
